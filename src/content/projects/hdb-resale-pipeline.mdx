## Approach

The pipeline loads 228,542 HDB resale records from data.gov.sg into Google Cloud Storage and BigQuery. 577 streets are
geocoded through OneMap. dbt models the data, with 24 tests guarding it. Kestra orchestrates the runs, Terraform
provisions the infrastructure, and Looker Studio serves the dashboard.
